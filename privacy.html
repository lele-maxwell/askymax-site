AskyMax Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026
Applies to: the AskyMax browser extension for Chrome and other Chromium browsers, and for Firefox
Formerly named: MaxAiChat
Developer: Maxwell

In short

  • AskyMax has no servers. The developer never receives your messages, your API key or anything you read.
  • What you ask, and any content you choose to include, goes directly from your browser to the AI provider you choose in Settings, and only when you send a message.
  • Your settings and chat history are stored only in your browser, on your device.
  • There are no analytics, no tracking, no advertising, and nothing is sold or shared.

What AskyMax stores, and where

Everything below is kept in your browser's private extension storage on your device. It is never uploaded or synced by AskyMax.

  • Provider settings: the provider, endpoint address, model and API key you enter. The key is stored as entered (not encrypted), like other browser data on your device.
  • Chat history: your questions, the answers, a chat title, the model used, and the title and address of the page a chat was about. The full text of pages, PDFs, video transcripts, emails and images is never saved. You can turn history off in Settings, delete single chats, or delete all chats.
  • Preferences: voice language and speed, read-aloud on/off, whether the page tab is shown, the panel width, which tips you have seen, and whether you agreed to the email notice.
  • Temporary data: while the panel is open, the extension keeps notes it made from a long document so it doesn't need to read it again. These are discarded when the panel or tab is closed.

What is sent, to whom, and when

AskyMax only reads or sends content when you act: sending a message, using a quick action, switching on the page chip, or attaching an image.

  • To your AI provider (Groq by default, an Ollama server on your own computer, or another OpenAI-compatible service you enter): your message, recent messages from the same chat, and, if you chose to include it, the content you asked about. That can be the text of the current web page or your selected text, a PDF's text (the PDF is downloaded to read it, up to 10 MB), a YouTube video's transcript, a Gmail email thread, or an image. Your API key is sent to that provider to authorise the request. Before an email thread is sent for the first time, AskyMax asks for your permission.
  • Your provider's own privacy policy governs what it does with that data. If you use Ollama, nothing leaves your computer.
  • Voice input uses your browser's built-in speech recognition. In Google Chrome, your browser sends the recorded audio to Google's speech service to turn it into text. AskyMax does not receive or keep the audio.
  • Read aloud uses your browser's built-in voices. Some browsers offer online voices provided by the browser maker.
  • Web pages you visit: to read a PDF, the extension downloads it from the address it was opened from. To read a YouTube transcript, it briefly opens and closes the video page's own transcript panel. To capture an image of the visible page, it takes a screenshot of the current tab, only when you choose "Capture the visible page".

AskyMax does not send data anywhere else.

Why the extension needs its permissions

  • Read and change data on websites and scripting: to read the page, PDF, transcript or email you ask about, and to show the AskyMax panel and its tab on pages.
  • Tabs and active tab: to know which page is open, so the right content and quick actions are offered.
  • Context menus: for "Ask AskyMax about this image" when you right-click an image.
  • Side panel (Chrome only): to show AskyMax on browser pages where the in-page panel can't appear.
  • Storage and unlimited storage: to keep your settings and chat history on your device without a size limit.

Your choices

  • Turn chat history off, delete single chats, or delete all chats in History.
  • Content from a page, PDF, video or email is only included when you ask for it.
  • Use Ollama to keep everything on your own computer.
  • Uninstalling AskyMax, or clearing your browser data, deletes everything it stored. Because nothing is kept anywhere else, it cannot be recovered afterwards.

Children

AskyMax is not directed at children under 13.

Changes to this policy

Changes are published in the extension's GitHub repository, with the date at the top of this page updated.

Chrome Web Store user data

AskyMax's use of information complies with the Chrome Web Store User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements: data is used only to provide the features you use, is not transferred to anyone other than the AI provider you choose, and is never used for advertising, credit decisions or any purpose unrelated to the extension.
